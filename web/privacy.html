Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 11, 2026

Channel Growth is a free, non-commercial, browser-based dashboard for viewing aggregate analytics for a YouTube channel that you own or manage. This policy explains what data the dashboard accesses, how it is used, and how it is stored.

Browser-only processing

Channel Growth has no backend application, user database, or server-side data store. When you connect a Google account, your browser requests analytics directly from Google's APIs and renders the results in the page. The operator of this site does not receive, collect, or store your Google user data.

Data the dashboard accesses

After you sign in with Google and grant consent, the dashboard reads data for the channel associated with your Google account using the channel==MINE reporting entity. It does not access data for any other channel.

  • Aggregate channel analytics: views, estimated watch time, and subscribers gained or lost per day.
  • Aggregate daily analytics for your top videos, used to draw per-video cumulative charts.
  • A content-type breakdown for the same metrics, such as Shorts and long-form videos.
  • Public video metadata: video IDs, titles, thumbnails, and content type for your top videos.
  • Public channel profile data: channel name, avatar, and current subscriber count.
  • Your uploads playlist metadata: video IDs, titles, thumbnails, durations, privacy status, scheduled publish times, and live/premiere start times.

The dashboard does not access viewer-level data, individual viewer identities, email addresses, or private information about your audience.

How access is authorized

Access is granted through Google Identity Services in your browser using Google OAuth 2.0 and these read-only scopes:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/yt-analytics.readonly
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly

You can revoke access at any time from the Google Account permissions page. Revoking access invalidates the token and prevents further API requests.

Storage and retention

The OAuth access token is kept in your browser tab's memory and in sessionStorage, so it survives a page refresh in the same tab. It is not sent to the operator of this site and is not stored in a cookie, localStorage, or any server-side system. The token expires after about one hour. No refresh token is issued to this application. The token is removed when you sign out, when it expires, or when you close the tab.

Analytics results are held only in page memory while you use the dashboard. Any CSV file you export is generated locally by your browser and saved according to your browser's download settings.

Cookies, analytics, and tracking

Channel Growth does not set its own cookies and does not use advertising, third-party analytics, or tracking pixels. The Chart.js library is served from this site rather than a third-party CDN. Google Identity Services is loaded from accounts.google.com only when you choose to sign in. Google may set cookies or process information as part of that sign-in flow under Google's Privacy Policy.

Sharing

Google user data accessed by Channel Growth is not sold, rented, or shared with advertisers or other third parties. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, so GitHub may process standard web-server logs such as IP addresses and request metadata as described in GitHub's privacy statement. Information may also be disclosed if required by law.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Channel Growth's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide the dashboard features described above, is not used for advertising, and is not sold or shared except as required by law.

Security

This site is served over HTTPS, requests only read-only scopes, and sends API requests directly from your browser to Google. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but the design minimizes exposure by keeping tokens and analytics out of server-side systems.

Children

This site is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the dashboard changes. The effective date at the top of this page will be revised when that happens.
